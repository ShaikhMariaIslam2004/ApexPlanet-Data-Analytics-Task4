## Hypothesis Testing

The hypothesis test focused on the relationship between Quantity and Total Sales.

### Hypotheses

H0: There is no statistically significant linear relationship between Quantity and Total Sales.

H1: There is a statistically significant linear relationship between Quantity and Total Sales.

The Pearson correlation test was performed using Python.

### Results

- Pearson correlation: 0.6466
- P-value: 1.71 × 10⁻¹¹⁹
- Significance level: 0.05
- 95% confidence interval: 0.609 to 0.681
- Sample size: 1,000

Since the p-value is much smaller than 0.05, the null hypothesis was rejected.

The result provides strong statistical evidence of a positive linear relationship between Quantity and Total Sales. This indicates an association and does not establish causation.

## Business Recommendations

1. Retain high-value customers by focusing retention efforts on Top Value customers, who contribute 55.27% of customer revenue.

2. Increase repeat purchases through follow-up offers and reminders, as most customers made only one purchase.

3. Prepare for weaker sales periods by investigating the August-September decline and planning campaigns in advance.

4. Focus on high-revenue products, particularly Laptop, which generated approximately ₹25.44M in revenue.
